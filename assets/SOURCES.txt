Unit War3 Claude: where the voices and portraits come from
==========================================================

The voices and portraits in this folder are original Warcraft III material
(c) Blizzard Entertainment. They are not covered by the MIT license of the
mod's code. They are redistributed here as packaged by the PeonPing project,
for non-commercial use, under the terms each pack declares.

Orc Peon / Орк-раб (assets/peon/)
  Voices: https://github.com/PeonPing/og-packs/tree/main/peon_ru
          pack peon_ru 1.1.0 by maksimfedin, declared license CC-BY-NC-4.0
  Portrait: https://github.com/PeonPing/peon-ping/blob/main/docs/peon-portrait.gif
          re-encoded from GIF to PNG, no visual edits.
          PeonPing repository license: PeonPing-LICENSE.txt (MIT, Tony Sheng).

Human Peasant / Крестьянин (assets/peasant/)
  Voices and portrait: https://github.com/PeonPing/og-packs/tree/main/peasant_ru
          pack peasant_ru 1.1.0 by maksimfedin, declared license CC-BY-NC-4.0
          portrait.png is the pack's peasant.png, no visual edits.

Undead Acolyte / Послушник (assets/acolyte/)
  Voices and portrait: https://github.com/PeonPing/og-packs/tree/main/acolyte_ru
          pack acolyte_ru 1.0.0 by maksimfedin, declared license CC-BY-NC-4.0
          portrait.png is the pack's acolyte.png, no visual edits.

Every WAV was checked against the SHA-256 listed in its pack's openpeon.json
when it was copied (2026-10-19 and 2026-10-20). Only the clips the mod plays
are included.

If you hold rights to any of this material and want it removed, open an issue
in the repository.
